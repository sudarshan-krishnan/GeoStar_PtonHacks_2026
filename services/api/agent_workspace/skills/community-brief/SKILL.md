# Community brief

1. Call `site_detail` for the site, then `local_conditions` with its latitude and longitude.
2. Cover, in this order: distance to homes and households within a mile, flood zone, water stress, climate,
   what happens in a grid emergency.
3. Write for a resident, not an engineer: no jargon, under 150 words, every figure from a tool with its source.
4. Make no claim about household electricity bills.
